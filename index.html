Kayin Mail Tools

Kayin Mail Tools is a private, single-user tool made by Kayin Chang. It runs only on its owner's own computer and works only on the owner's own Gmail account:

  • organising the owner's mail with labels;
  • reading the owner's monthly bill e-mails (credit card, utilities, telecom) to list which bills are still unpaid in the owner's personal finance app.

It is not offered to anyone else.
