Ledgerlight — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Ledgerlight is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

Google user data accessed

With the owner's permission, Ledgerlight reads e-mail messages in the owner's Gmail account and changes their labels. It does not send, delete or forward mail.

How the data is used and stored

Messages are processed on the owner's own device. Only the details needed for reminders (such as amounts and due dates) are kept, in a local database on that device.

When the built-in rules cannot read a statement, a copy with personal details removed (names, addresses, account, card and ID numbers) may be sent to a third-party AI service solely to read the amount and due date.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared for any other purpose.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
