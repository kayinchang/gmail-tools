Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

This is a private, single-user tool used only by its owner on the owner's own Gmail account.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission it reads Gmail messages and, for labelling, changes their labels. For bills it reads only bill e-mails and their attachments.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Extracted bill details (payee, amount, due date) are stored in the owner's local database.

When the tool's own rules cannot read a bill e-mail, a copy with personal details (names, addresses, account and card numbers, ID numbers) removed is sent to an AI model (Anthropic Claude) only to read the amount and due date.

Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared for any other purpose.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
